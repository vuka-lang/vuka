# E0370: A deferred group's section is misplaced

`<ui.Await key="group">` shows one of a live component's
[deferred groups](/ui/stateful#await): its `<slot:pending>` while the group
loads, its `<slot:error>` once the loader failed, else its content. It
takes a `key`, the group's name, and those two slots. Reported by the
compiler:

- a `<ui.Await>` in the markup of a component that isn't live, which has no
  deferred groups;
- a `<ui.Await>` without a `key`, or a `key` that isn't a name;
- another attribute on the tag, another slot, a second one;
- a `let` on `<slot:pending>`, or one naming more than the error on
  `<slot:error>`.

A `key` naming no group the component defers is
[E0325](/errors/E0325).

## Wrong

```html
<ui.Await key="orders" class="box">
  <slot:loading><ui.Skeleton lines={3}/></slot:loading>
  <slot:error let={err, n}>{err.Error()}</slot:error>
  <OrderList orders={orders} />
</ui.Await>
```

## Right

```html
<div class="box">
  <ui.Await key="orders">
    <slot:pending><ui.Skeleton lines={3}/></slot:pending>
    <slot:error let={err}>{err.Error()}</slot:error>
    <OrderList orders={orders} />
  </ui.Await>
</div>
```
