# E0724: The home page outside the root layouts

With no `pages/_layout.vuka.html`, each route group at the top of `pages/`
may have a `_layout.vuka.html` of its own: the document of the pages inside
it, a root layout. The site's sections then each have their own document,
and the home page, `/`, belongs to one of them. A home page outside every
group with a layout would render with no document of the site's at all.

## Wrong

```text
pages/
  index.vuka.html              Home — /, under no root layout
  (site)/
    _layout.vuka.html          Site: the document of the site's pages
    about.vuka.html            About — /about
  (shop)/
    _layout.vuka.html          Shop: the document of the shop's pages
    cart.vuka.html             Cart — /cart
```

## Right

```text
pages/
  (site)/
    _layout.vuka.html          Site: the document of the site's pages
    index.vuka.html            Home — /
    about.vuka.html            About — /about
  (shop)/
    _layout.vuka.html          Shop: the document of the shop's pages
    cart.vuka.html             Cart — /cart
```
